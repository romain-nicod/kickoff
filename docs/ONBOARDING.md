# Onboarding

Someone joins mid-project. Target: **productive in half a day**, not on
day three.

```bash
git clone git@github.com:{{REPO}}.git
cd {{REPO_NAME}}
```

## Hour 2 — the product

In this order, and no other:

1. [`README.md`](../README.md) — what it is, how it runs.
2. [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) — the absolute rules and the known traps.
   **The one document to actually read**, not skim.
3. The design reference, opened and looked at for five minutes.
4. The specification: the epic overview and the batching — what gets
   built and in what order.

The business rules are reference material, not reading material. They
are consulted while implementing.

## Hour 3 — the way of working

- [`GOLDEN_RULES.md`](../GOLDEN_RULES.md) — read once, then used as a
  review checklist
- [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md) — branches, commits, reviews
- [`DOR_DOD.md`](../DOR_DOD.md) — when a story starts, when it is done
- [`BOARD.md`](BOARD.md) — the columns and their rules

## Hour 4 — the first contribution

Take a **2-point story in the current batch**, not a 5, not the hardest
part. The goal is to go through the whole loop once — branch, code, PR,
review, merge, Done — while someone is available to unblock you.

Your buddy for the first day: _to fill in_. One name, so that "who do I
ask" is never a question.
