## The rules that make hats useful

**A decision is taken in the meeting, not after it.** If the hat's owner
is present, they decide on the spot. If not, the decision waits or
someone else takes it explicitly and says so.

**A hat can be handed over, never abandoned.** Say it in the standup.

**Nobody works alone on the critical path.** Identify the two or three
places where a solo blocker costs the demonstration, and pair on them.
